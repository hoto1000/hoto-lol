Privacy

I don't want your data. There are no accounts and no forms, and nothing you type here is sent to this site.

Ads

This site does not currently show ads. If that changes, this page will be updated before any ads appear.

Civic guide

The civic guide has no ads, trackers, or analytics. It blocks all network requests, so your answers cannot be sent anywhere. Answers are kept only in the open page, never saved to your browser, and disappear when you leave or press “Reset all answers.” Your political answers are never used for ads, accounts, or retargeting.

Podcast audio

Episodes are hosted on this site and only load when you press Play.

Fonts

The fonts are stored on this site too. Showing text never contacts a font service or any other outside server.
