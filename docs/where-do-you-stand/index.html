Where do you stand?

Thirty statements about sharing, work, home, care, power and hope. Pick "That's me," "Not sure" or "Not me" for each, or skip it. At the end you'll see the ideas you already agree with.

I'll be honest about what this is. This blog takes a side, and these questions come from that side. I think a lot of people already believe in sharing, fair work and care for everyone, and just haven't heard it said out loud. You might not, and that's okay too.

There's no score, no label, and no wrong answer. Nothing is typed in, saved or sent.
