Civic guide

An independent civic tool for seeing where you stand on the issues, in your own words.

What it will be

  • Neutral. Every issue is described the way its own supporters and critics would describe it.
  • Private. Your answers stay on your device. Nothing is sent, saved, or sold.
  • Independent. Not affiliated with any party, candidate, campaign, or government.
  • Honest. It shows you what you think. It does not tell you how to vote.
