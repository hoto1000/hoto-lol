What does taking part in public life look like in my city this week?

Philadelphia protests and rallies, late October to early November

Oct 27, 2026 · 2 min read

This is a plain list of public protests, rallies, and vigils in Philadelphia over the next couple of weeks, as listed on public event sites on October 27, 2026. It is a list, not an endorsement. Most of what shows up on these sites comes from progressive and anti-war groups, so it is not the whole range of political views in the city. If you know of events from other perspectives, the format here would work for them too.

Why this list

Peaceful assembly is a protected right, and showing up is one of the oldest ways people take part in public life. Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, and several groups are pushing early voting and poll-watcher sign-ups. The Philadelphia Inquirer covered the October 17 “No Kings” rally at City Hall, which centered on turnout.

Dated events

  • Tue Oct 27, 12:30 PM: Save Our Homes Now, a rally about neighborhood restoration homes (One Pennsylvania).
  • Wed Oct 28, 7:00 PM: NO ICE Philly general meeting, Center City (immigrant rights).
  • Thu Oct 29, 8:00 AM: No Kings Vigil at Pennsylvania Ave and Spring Garden St (Indivisible).
  • Thu Oct 29, 7:00 PM: De-Escalation 101 training, Bella Vista (The Wooden Shoe).
  • Fri Oct 30, 11:15 AM: Weekly vigil, Fridays@Fetterman’s (Palestine, anti-war).
  • Fri Oct 30, 12:00 PM: Action Hour for Palestine, online option (American Friends Service Committee).
  • Sat Oct 31, 9:00 AM: Runners for Justice in Palestine (Families for Ceasefire Philly).
  • Mon Nov 2, 6:30 AM: NO ICE noise demo, Chinatown (NO ICE Philly).
  • Tue Nov 3, 6:30 AM: D&Z Out of Philly (Day & Zimmerman Out of Philly).
  • Sat Nov 7, 11:00 AM: Hands Off Our Vote, Philadelphia delegation (One Pennsylvania).

Repeating events

  • No Kings Vigil at 23rd and Spring Garden, on many days through early November, usually late afternoon.
  • Mondays at noon: “Mondays @ McCormick’s,” 2000 Market St (Peacehome Campaigns).
  • Tuesdays at 6:30 AM: D&Z Out of Philly. Saturdays at 9 AM: Runners for Justice in Palestine.

What is uncertain

These sites rely on organizers submitting their own events, so times and places change and some events are missing. Check the organizer’s own page before you go. I have not confirmed any single event with its organizer.

If you go

Protests can be unpredictable. Many people find it helpful to tell someone where they are going, bring water, charge a phone, and know where the exits are. Training sessions like the de-escalation one above exist for exactly this reason.

Sources

Event details come from Find a Protest, Philadelphia (updated October 23, 2026) and Mobilize data via stallman.org/mobilize. Election context comes from the Philadelphia Inquirer, October 18, 2026.
