Open questions

Things I am still thinking through. No essay yet, and no deadline.

  • Who pays the paperwork tax?

    Forms, deadlines and repeat documents make people spend hours just to reach help. Which step could we remove?

  • Can a bus route decide who gets care?

    A service only helps the people who can actually reach it.

  • Who gets heard at a public meeting?

    Meeting times, childcare, transit, disability access and language decide who is in the room.

  • Is the public library part of the safety net?

    Internet, job searches, a cool room and company, without pretending it can replace housing or health care.

  • Who does the hidden second shift?

    School forms, appointments and office hours quietly assume someone has time for unpaid care.

  • What does a second chance need?

    One practical barrier after incarceration, like a housing application, without reducing anyone to their record.

How I argue here

Rules I hold myself to, so you can see where fact ends and opinion begins.

  • Opinion is labeled.

    When a piece is my view, it says so. Opinion pieces are marked on the index.

  • Claims get sources.

    Facts link to where they come from. Uncertain parts are marked as uncertain.

  • Disagreement is welcome.

    The strongest fair objection belongs inside the essay, not outside it.

  • Errors get fixed.

    If something turns out wrong, I correct it at the top of the post, with the date and what changed.
