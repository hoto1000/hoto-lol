A number worth knowing

Okay, quick. What number do you call for a fire? 911. Easy.

Now what do you call when your mind is the emergency?

Most people don’t know. In a 2023 survey, only 13% of U.S. adults had heard of 988.

I’m a therapy grad student, and that still gets to me. It’s free and confidential. You can call or text 988, or chat at 988lifeline.org, 24/7.

Help like this rarely has money for ads. So it spreads the way good things usually spread. One person tells another.

So I’m telling you, and I hope you tell one more person.

Check it yourself

  • 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline
  • The Pew Charitable Trusts, national survey on 988 awareness, conducted with Ipsos (2023): 13% of U.S. adults had heard of 988.

Student writing, not clinical advice. If you are struggling, a licensed professional or a local crisis line can help.
